## 4. Official Judging Criteria & Exact Weights

Every submission is evaluated against **four predetermined, fixed questions** comprising 100% of the project score:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                      OFFICIAL JUDGING MATRIX                           │
├────────────────────────────────────────┬────────┬──────────────────────┤
│ Criterion                              │ Weight │ Focus Area           │
├────────────────────────────────────────┼────────┼──────────────────────┤
│ 1. Does it solve a real problem in     │  30%   │ Authenticity, user   │
│    your work?                          │        │ clarity, retention   │
├────────────────────────────────────────┼────────┼──────────────────────┤
│ 2. Was Wispr used meaningfully?        │  30%   │ Voice integration,   │
│    (Flow throughout build & workflow)  │        │ usage proof, demo    │
├────────────────────────────────────────┼────────┼──────────────────────┤
│ 3. Does it work?                       │  25%   │ Live testable link,  │
│    (Functionality & accessibility)     │        │ zero friction        │
├────────────────────────────────────────┼────────┼──────────────────────┤
│ 4. Is the demo clear?                  │  15%   │ Concise <= 2 min     │
│    (High-signal video walkthrough)     │        │ story & proof        │
└────────────────────────────────────────┴────────┴──────────────────────┘
```

### Deep-Dive on Criteria:

#### 1. Real Problem in Your Work (30% Weight)
- Is this an authentic problem that actually causes friction, delays, or repetitive fatigue in your real work?
- Is there an unmistakable target persona (who exactly is this for)?
- Would the creator or their team continue using this tool every week after the hackathon ends?
- *Tip for Agents:* Avoid speculative, generic toys (e.g., another generic crypto dashboard or simple todo app). Focus on painful micro-workflows: repetitive email replies, tedious spreadsheet wrangling, manual CRM synchronization, messy meeting-to-ticket conversion, or compliance checks.

#### 2. Meaningful Wispr Flow Usage (30% Weight)
- Was Wispr Flow genuinely woven into the creation of the tool and the operational execution?
- Did you speak your system prompts, specs, feature requests, documentation, and user stories into your AI agents/builders?
- Does your video demo visually highlight you holding the shortcut key, speaking instructions naturally, and watching Flow stream clean text without filler words?
- Does your submitted Wispr usage card validate high word counts during the hackathon period?

#### 3. Functional Product (25% Weight)
- The judges must be able to click your URL and immediately interact with the product.
- **Zero friction:** No mandatory login wall requiring payment, custom approvals, or private credentials. If authentication is mandatory, pre-fill sample credentials and supply ready-to-test mock datasets right on the home screen.
- The product must reliably execute its promised workflow from input to output.

#### 4. Clear Demo Video (15% Weight)
- Maximum length: **2 minutes** (e.g., 1:45 to 1:55 is the sweet spot).
- Hosted publicly or as an unlisted link on YouTube / Loom / Vimeo.
- Structure:
  1. **Hook & Problem (0:00 - 0:25):** The pain point in your daily work.
  2. **The Build & Voice Interaction (0:25 - 1:00):** Show Wispr Flow in action dictating instructions/prompts into Cursor/Lovable/Claude.
  3. **The Working Tool Demo (1:00 - 1:40):** Run real data through the live tool and show the output.
  4. **Value & Impact (1:40 - 2:00):** Summary of time saved and ongoing adoption.

## 5. Fairness Guarantees & Evaluation Process

WeMakeDevs and Wispr Flow implemented a 7-point fairness charter:

1. **Fixed Weights:** The criteria and weights (30%, 30%, 25%, 15%) were locked prior to challenge commencement.
2. **Multi-Judge Averaging:** Every submission is evaluated by multiple independent judges. The final score is the mathematical average.
3. **Public Scores:** All individual scores given by every judge are displayed openly on the leaderboard.
4. **Written Feedback:** Every project receives personalized, written commentary from judges detailing strengths and specific improvement areas.
5. **Conflict of Interest Shield:** Judges are disqualified from grading any project with which they have personal, familial, or direct business associations.
6. **24-Hour Appeal Window:** Scores and comments are released on Wednesday, 4 November. Builders have until Thursday, 5 November 11:59 PM IST to submit queries or contest oversights with a guaranteed written reply before final winner confirmation.
7. **Usage Verification:** Top Wispr usage metrics are verified against internal telemetry with Wispr engineers before prizes are awarded.

- **Voice integration proof:** Specify the shortcut trigger used (e.g., "hold Fn and talk") and show iterative refinements dictated by voice.

## 9. Wispr Flow Tooling & Voice-First Workflow Guide

### What is Wispr Flow?
Wispr Flow is a next-generation desktop dictation and voice-first productivity tool for Mac and Windows.
- **Core Mechanism:** Hold a shortcut key (e.g., `Fn` on Mac or configured hotkey), speak naturally in your normal conversational rhythm, and release.
- **Instant Transformation:** Automatically eliminates filler words (*"um"*, *"like"*, *"you know"*, *"okay so"*), handles repetitions, detects sentence boundaries, inserts appropriate punctuation, and shapes prose to fit the target application.
- **Speed:** Delivers polished text directly at the cursor location **up to 4x faster than standard typing**.
- **Context-Aware:** Adapts to the context of the active window (code editor, terminal, browser, document, chat app).

### Hackathon Pro Setup Instructions
1. Download Wispr Flow for macOS or Windows: [wisprflow.ai](https://wisprflow.ai).
2. Apply the official hackathon promo code: `WEMAKEDEVS`.
3. Unlock **1 Month of Wispr Flow Pro free**.
4. Configure your hotkey in `Preferences` (e.g., double-tap or hold `Fn`, `Caps Lock`, or `Option`).
5. Verify your microphone input and enable accessibility/dictation permissions.
6. Open `Insights` → `Your usage` to monitor your daily word count progression.

### Optimal Dictation Workflow for Builders:
```
[Identify Task] ──► [Hold Wispr Shortcut] ──► [Dictate Spec / Code Prompt] 
                                                      │
[Clean Polished Prompt Injected into IDE/App] ◄───────┘
         │
[Agent Generates Code / Workflow] ──► [Dictate Voice Refinements / Bugs] ──► [Ship]
```

- **Voice for Thought:** Use voice for specs, user stories, architecture questions, bug reports, and PR descriptions.
- **Keyboard for Precision:** Use keyboard for navigation shortcuts, syntax triggers, and minor character edits.

## 10. Full Official FAQ (Verbatim Reference)

### Q1: What is No Hands?
> **Answer:** A two-week online hackathon from WeMakeDevs and Wispr Flow, from 16 to 30 October. Build a tool that solves a problem in your own work, and use Wispr Flow to speak your prompts, instructions and docs as you build. [Get 1 month of Wispr Flow Pro free](https://wisprflow.ai/?promo_code=WEMAKEDEVS&utm_source=wemakedevs&utm_medium=partner&utm_campaign=wemakedevs&dub_id=1oU35MQWSE7nktLN).

### Q2: Who can take part?
> **Answer:** Anyone in India. Professionals across all domains are welcome: founders, developers, marketing, product, HR and others. Students are welcome as well. It's a solo challenge, so no teams: you register, set up Wispr and build on your own.

### Q3: Do I need to know how to code?
> **Answer:** No. The No-Code track is built for people who don't write code, whatever your role: marketing, sales, HR, ops, design or anything else. You build your tool with an AI app builder like Lovable or an assistant like ChatGPT or Claude, and tell it what to do by talking through Wispr Flow.

### Q4: What counts as a No-Code entry?
> **Answer:** A tool someone else can open and use. An app made with an AI app builder like Lovable counts, and so does a reusable assistant, such as a custom GPT or a Gemini Gem shared by link, or a workflow in Zapier, Make or Notion that runs when the judges trigger it, from a form for example. A chat thread or a one-off prompt doesn't count. Judges need to try it without paying or asking you for access: share a public link, and if it needs a sign-in, put a test login and sample input in your description.

### Q5: Does it cost anything?
> **Answer:** No. The challenge is free to enter, and your Wispr Flow link includes 1 month of Wispr Flow Pro free.

### Q6: What do I need?
> **Answer:** A Mac or Windows computer with a microphone, and a Wispr Flow account.

### Q7: Why do I set up Wispr before I register?
> **Answer:** Because the challenge is about solving problems in a voice-first way. Your prompts, specs and docs are spoken, not typed, so Flow has to be working before you start building.

### Q8: Do I have to use only my voice?
> **Answer:** Use your voice for as much as you can: prompts, docs, messages and commit notes. You can still use the keyboard for shortcuts and small fixes. Judges look at how much of your writing went through Flow.

### Q9: Can I use other tools?
> **Answer:** Yes. Use any editor, AI assistant or app builder you like. Wispr Flow is how you talk to them.

### Q10: Does the free month cover the other tools?
> **Answer:** No. The free month is Wispr Flow Pro only. Lovable, Cursor, ChatGPT, Claude and the rest need your own accounts. Their free plans are enough for the examples on this page, but they cap how many messages or credits you get a day, so start early. You don't have to pay for anything to take part.

### Q11: Can I enter more than one track?
> **Answer:** No. Pick one track, Builders or No-Code, and submit one project in it.

### Q12: How is Wispr usage measured?
> **Answer:** From the usage card in your Wispr Flow desktop app, under Insights and Your usage. Winners' numbers are confirmed with Wispr before prizes are sent.

### Q13: What if I disagree with my score?
> **Answer:** Every score and comment is public. You can raise a question until the end of Thursday 5 November and you will get a written reply before winners are confirmed.

### Q14: When are prizes sent?
> **Answer:** Prize money is sent on Friday 6 November, the day winners are announced. Weekly prizes ship the day weekly winners are named, to an address in India.

### Q15: Who owns what I build?
> **Answer:** You do. WeMakeDevs and Wispr have the right to feature your project and demo video on their channels.

### Q16: Can I join after 16 October?
> **Answer:** Yes. Registration stays open until submissions close on Friday 30 October.

| **Meaningful Wispr Usage** (30%) | **30/30** | Record a demo clearly showing Wispr Flow active. Document the prompts spoken via Flow in the README. Keep high cumulative word counts on the desktop Insights card. Dictate every commit message and PR description. |
| **Does it Work?** (25%) | **25/25** | Deploy to a zero-friction URL (Vercel, Lovable, Cloudflare). Ensure 1-click testability. Preload sample data so a judge can click "Run Sample" and see the immediate output without having to type or register. |
| **Clear Demo Video** (15%) | **15/15** | Keep video under 1 minute 55 seconds. No fluff intro. Jump straight to: Problem (20s) → Wispr Voice Build Workflow (40s) → Live Running Demo (40s) → Real Result Produced (15s). |
